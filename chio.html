BEFORE ANY REAL MONEY

  • The test period (not the train period) made money after fees, with 30 or more trades.
  • It beat simply holding, or had a much smaller drawdown than holding.
  • You can stomach the max drawdown it showed, in real money, without panicking.
  • You paper traded it live for at least 4 weeks and results looked like the backtest.
  • You risk 1% or less per trade, and only money you can afford to lose completely.
  • Start tiny. Scale up only after real results match paper results.
